How I work

Customers rarely come with a purely technical problem. They come with a business problem that shows up as technical pain, running on their infrastructure.

My job is to find that problem, understand what it costs them, and turn it into a measurable technical solution that both business and engineering stakeholders understand: higher ROI for the business, less friction for the engineers.

  1. Find the real pain

    What's slow, blocked or breaking, and why.

  2. Name the business gap

    Find the real business costs.

  3. Design the answer

    Architecture, PoC and a rollout that fits.

  4. Bring everyone along

    Biz & Dev aligned, through to production.
